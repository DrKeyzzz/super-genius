How to play

  1. The dealer plays their card, then a mystery note. Cards flip over: pick the card that shows the mystery note (click or tap it, or press 1 to 4).
  2. Right answers win chips, and a streak multiplies them. Wrong answers cost a chip. Every town ends at High Noon: beat the Interval Outlaw to stamp their Wanted poster.
  3. Press your luck: after each town, cash out to keep your chips as your score, or ride on for bigger stakes. Go bust and you keep only half your best.

Stuck on an interval? Hank's Songbook plays each one with a song you know. In Practice there are no chips and no clock.
